## PDR-S0-8 — Does framework scope equal pilot scope?

> **Status:** drafted · **Layer:** 2 · **Strand:** S0 — Scope
> **Requirements tested against:** R8, R17
> **Opens:** PDR-S0-2 (property types), PDR-S0-7 (data categories)

## Question

The Product & Service Design workstream scopes certain property types and situations out of the pilot. Does the framework specification inherit those exclusions, or is its scope determined separately?

## Why this is drafted first

It is not on any workstream's question list, and it is cheap to get wrong by inattention rather than by disagreement. Exclusions that are sensible for a pilot — narrowing to make delivery tractable — become permanent properties of a specification if they are inherited without anyone deciding to inherit them.

The two questions it opens, PDR-S0-2 and PDR-S0-7, cannot be framed until it resolves, because both are conditional on whether pilot scope binds the specification.
