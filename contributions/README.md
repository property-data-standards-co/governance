# Contributions

Written contributions from this record to other workstreams' sessions: notes that
apply the requirements and decision map to material another workstream has tabled.

They are coalition material and held to the same neutrality checks as every other
document here (`npm run check`). They are not part of the record: nothing in them is
decided, and a contribution does not open or close a decision.
