## Why both permit commercial use and derivative works

A standard that forbids commercial use cannot be implemented by the market it
governs. A standard that forbids derivative works cannot be forked if its
custodian stops maintaining it, which makes every implementer dependent on the
continued goodwill of a single body.

"Free to use" does not settle either question — it is satisfied by licences that
forbid both. Terms are stated explicitly here for that reason.
